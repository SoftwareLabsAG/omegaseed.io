1. The essentials first

This website does not process the content you enter.

Words, codes and characters you type into the tool never leave your browser. There is no transmission to a server, no interim storage and no logging of this content. All calculations run exclusively on your device. No cookies are set, no analytics services are used and no resources are loaded from third-party servers.

3. What happens to your input

The tool on this website accepts words or character strings and converts them into an arrangement of tiles. This processing takes place entirely in the memory of your browser (client-side, in JavaScript). No processing by the controller within the meaning of the GDPR takes place, because the data never reaches the controller.

Closing or reloading the page discards this input. There is no recovery function — not even for the operator.

A word in our own cause. Even though nothing is transmitted technically: a browser on a device connected to the internet is not a safe environment for real key material. For real use, the offline version on a network-isolated computer is what is intended.

4. Server log files

When the website is accessed, the hosting provider automatically records data in server log files which your browser transmits. This is technically unavoidable and concerns:

  • IP address of the requesting device
  • date and time of access
  • name and URL of the file retrieved
  • volume of data transferred and notification of the retrieval status
  • browser type, browser version and operating system
  • where applicable, the page previously visited (referrer)
Purpose
delivery of the website, ensuring operational security, defending against and investigating attacks, error analysis
Legal basis
Art 6(1)(f) GDPR — legitimate interest in secure and trouble-free operation. This data is not combined with other data sources; it is not evaluated for marketing or profiling purposes.
Processor
DigitalOcean, LLC, 105 Edgeview Drive, Suite 425, Broomfield, CO 80021, USA. A data processing agreement pursuant to Art 28 GDPR is in place with DigitalOcean; it is concluded upon acceptance of the terms of service and incorporates the European Commission's standard contractual clauses. See section 9 for details.
Retention period
14 days; beyond that only where a specific security incident needs to be investigated. The log files are then deleted automatically.

5. Local storage, cookies

This website sets no cookies.

It uses your browser's local storage (localStorage) for at most two entries:

KeyContentPurposeDuration
omegaseedphrase.hinweise.v1 1 records that the opening notices have been read — only after explicit consent until you clear your browser storage
omegaseedphrase.theme light / dark records the chosen appearance if you use the switch in the page header until you clear your browser storage

Neither entry contains personal data, an identifier or a timestamp. They are not transmitted to any server. If you never switch the appearance, the page follows your device's system setting and stores nothing for it.

Legal basis
Art 6(1)(a) GDPR (consent) and § 165 para 3 Austrian Telecommunications Act 2021. If you decline, nothing is stored; the website works unchanged and the notices reappear on your next visit. The appearance entry is created solely by your own switching and is necessary for the function you requested (§ 165 para 3 no 2 TKG 2021).
Withdrawal
You can remove the entry at any time via your browser settings („clear site data“). Withdrawal takes effect for the future.

6. No external resources

All components of the website — fonts, images, program code and the BIP39 word list — are delivered from the same server as the page itself. In particular no Google Fonts, no content delivery networks, no analytics tools, no social media plugins, no map services and no ad networks are embedded. Accessing the page therefore creates no connections to further third parties beyond the hosting provider named in section 9.

7. Offline version

The offline version offered for download is a single HTML file without network functions. After downloading, no communication with the provider takes place — neither update queries nor usage statistics. The file stores nothing, not even the acknowledgement of the notices.

8. Contacting us

If you write to us by e-mail, we process the data you provide (sender address, content, name where applicable) in order to handle your enquiry.

Legal basis
Art 6(1)(b) GDPR for pre-contractual or contractual enquiries, otherwise Art 6(1)(f) GDPR (legitimate interest in responding)
Retention period
until the enquiry has been dealt with conclusively, thereafter at most within the scope of statutory retention obligations (in particular § 132 BAO, § 212 UGB: seven years)

Never send a seed phrase by e-mail. We never ask for seed phrases, private keys or passwords. Any message that does is not from us.

The website is operated at DigitalOcean, LLC, 105 Edgeview Drive, Suite 425, Broomfield, CO 80021, USA. DigitalOcean processes exclusively the server log files described in section 4, as a processor under Art 28 GDPR; the data processing agreement is concluded upon acceptance of the terms of service.

The domain is registered with GoDaddy. Resolving the domain name sends queries to the name servers operated there. This generates technical connection data required to establish the connection. No access to website content or to your input takes place in the process.

Other recipients

Beyond this, personal data is disclosed only to authorities and courts where a statutory obligation exists. Data is not sold. There is no automated decision-making, including profiling, within the meaning of Art 22 GDPR.
